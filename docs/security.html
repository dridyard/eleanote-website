Privacy & Security

At Eleanote, we take the privacy and security of our users and their patients seriously. The transcription and AI partners we rely on are bound by Business Associate Agreements that require them to safeguard protected health information (PHI) in accordance with HIPAA. Eleanote itself doesn't use patient information to train AI models, and doesn't store audio, transcripts, or notes on its servers. Eleanote has also been independently reviewed by a third party to evaluate the safeguards in place to protect this information — documentation is linked below.
